## Main Storylines (Tension-Driven)

### Storyline 1: The Magic Ticket Trail

**Tensions:** 1 (Hidden Spending), 3 (Emergency Fund Fiction)
**Time range:** November 2025 – April 2026 (ongoing)

**What happens:** Eric buys Orlando Magic tickets throughout the NBA season — sometimes with Trevor, sometimes solo. He tells Vanessa certain tickets were free from a coworker or cheaper than they were. Ticketmaster confirmations pile up in his email. Trevor occasionally texts about upcoming games in ways that could reveal frequency. Each purchase chips further into the savings account Eric is misrepresenting.

**Servers touched:**
- **ticketmaster** — 2–3 ticket purchases per month ($60–120 each), occasional concert ticket, some transfers to/from Trevor
- **email** — Ticketmaster order confirmations, occasional Stubhub receipts, soccer tournament fee invoices
- **messaging** — Trevor texts about upcoming games ("yo u coming saturday??"); Eric texts Vanessa that "Trevor had an extra ticket" or "it was a work appreciation thing"
- **calendar** — Some game nights appear on the shared calendar; others are deliberately omitted

**Cross-server links:**
- Ticketmaster purchase dates must match email confirmation dates
- Trevor's "u coming tmrw??" texts must align with actual game dates on the Magic schedule
- Eric's messaging to Vanessa ("free ticket from work") must contradict the Ticketmaster purchase record showing $85 charged
- Calendar game entries should appear for only some purchased tickets — the ones Eric didn't hide

### Storyline 2: The Community Garden Gauntlet

**Tensions:** 2 (Social Anxiety), 4 (Committee on the Clock)
**Time range:** January – April 2026

**What happens:** The community garden committee ramps up for spring planting season. Trevor pushes Eric to take on a bigger organizational role. Eric starts doing more committee work during clinic hours using Airtable. Two committee meetings trigger anxiety spirals — one he cancels last minute, the other he attends but his Apple Watch records a heart rate spike to 125 bpm. Late-night Strava rides follow both incidents.

**Servers touched:**
- **airtable** — Committee volunteer tracking, plot assignment spreadsheets, spring event planning (entries created or edited between 9 AM – 5 PM)
- **calendar** — Committee meeting invites, "Patient Documentation" blocks that overlap with committee email timestamps, cancelled meeting entries
- **email** — Committee threads (garden@orlandocommunitygardens.org) sent during business hours, event announcements
- **messaging** — Trevor pushing Eric to attend ("bro just show up for 20 min 💪"); Eric making excuses ("might be stuck at the clinic late")
- **apple_health** — Heart rate spikes (110–125 bpm resting) during committee meetings, elevated resting HR on meeting days
- **strava** — Late-night cycling sessions (9:30–11 PM, 8–12 miles) on committee meeting days
- **reminder** — "Breathing exercises before garden meeting" and "You don't have to stay the whole time" entries

**Cross-server links:**
- Calendar "Patient Documentation" blocks must overlap with Airtable committee entry edit timestamps AND committee email send times
- Apple Health heart rate spikes must correlate with calendar committee meeting start times
- Strava late-night rides must fall on the same days as committee meetings or planned social events
- Cancelled calendar events must align with excuse texts to Trevor in messaging
- Reminder self-care entries must appear the morning after Strava late-night rides

### Storyline 3: Omar's Quarterly Reality Check

**Tensions:** 3 (Emergency Fund Fiction), 1 (Hidden Spending)
**Time range:** January 2026 and April 2026 (two quarterly cycles)

**What happens:** Omar sends monthly email summaries of Eric's financial picture and schedules two quarterly in-person meetings. His emails reference the real savings balance (~$1,200) and recommend cutting discretionary spending. Eric reviews these privately and sets reminders to transfer money to savings before Vanessa notices. The gap between Omar's numbers and what Eric tells Vanessa is starkly visible across the data.

**Servers touched:**
- **email** — Omar's monthly financial summaries with real balances, quarterly meeting confirmations, savings rate recommendations
- **calendar** — Quarterly financial planning sessions (January 15, April 16)
- **messaging** — Eric texting Vanessa about their finances within days of receiving Omar's contradicting figures ("we're at about four grand babe, we're okay")
- **reminder** — "Transfer $200 to savings before Friday" and "Review Omar's summary before meeting" entries

**Cross-server links:**
- Omar's email balance figures (~$1,200 savings) must be within the same week as Eric's messaging claims to Vanessa (~$4,000)
- Calendar check-in dates must match the email scheduling threads
- Reminder "transfer to savings" entries should appear 1–3 days after Omar's monthly emails
- Eric's reassuring texts to Vanessa must fall within days of Omar's emails showing the real numbers

### Storyline 4: The LinkedIn Scroll

**Tensions:** 5 (Quiet Job Search)
**Time range:** February – April 2026

**What happens:** Eric starts receiving LinkedIn job alerts after quietly updating his profile in early February. A recruiter from BayCare Health, Rachel Moran, reaches out about a Community Health Outreach Coordinator position. Eric responds cautiously. He has one phone screen scheduled as a "Personal Appointment" on his calendar. He adds Rachel's contact to his phone. He tells nobody.

**Servers touched:**
- **email** — LinkedIn job alert emails (weekly starting early February), recruiter exchange with Rachel Moran (3–4 messages over two weeks in late February / early March), BayCare job listing notification
- **calendar** — "Personal Appointment" on March 12 at 12:30 PM (lunch break phone screen)
- **contacts** — Rachel Moran added to phone in mid-February with only name and email — no relationship label

**Cross-server links:**
- LinkedIn alert emails must start in early February and arrive weekly
- Rachel Moran's first email must precede the contact creation date by no more than a few days
- The calendar "Personal Appointment" must fall within two weeks of the email exchange
- No messaging content references the job search — the silence across messaging is the tell
